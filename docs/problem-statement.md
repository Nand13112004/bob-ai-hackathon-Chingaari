# Problem statement

Grid reliability depends on early detection of equipment deterioration. Transformers are critical assets in the distribution network, and unplanned failures can cause widespread outages, customer disruption, and expensive emergency repair work.

Utilities have traditionally relied on reactive maintenance and periodic checks, but those methods are limited when failures are driven by subtle sensor drift, weather stress, and historical operating patterns. A predictive system that combines transformer metadata, sensor time-series, weather, maintenance history, and incident information helps operators move from reactive repair to proactive intervention.

The value of predictive maintenance is clear: it reduces outage duration, protects essential customer segments, improves crew planning, and prevents avoidable emergency dispatches.
